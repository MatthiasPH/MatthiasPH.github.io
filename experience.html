3D Printing & Additive ManufacturingIndependent projects · Design for FDM · Prototyping
+

Over six years and 1,500+ hours of 3D printing, I have developed strong practical knowledge of additive manufacturing, material selection and designing parts specifically for the process. I use 3D printing to produce functional prototypes and end-use components for mechanical and electromechanical projects, not just visual models.

My design work considers print orientation, loading direction, dimensional tolerances, fit, wall thickness, support requirements and the trade-offs between strength, weight, finish and print time. I select and tune printing materials and settings to suit each part's intended function.

I am comfortable calibrating printers, diagnosing print defects, improving bed adhesion and dimensional accuracy, and iterating designs based on assembly and mechanical testing. This experience helps me turn CAD models into reliable, manufacturable components quickly.

02 / ABOUT ME

Curious by design.

I enjoy the entire engineering process — from figuring out how something should work to making a prototype and improving it.

I'm most interested in the point where mechanical design meets the real world. I like making things, taking them apart, figuring out why they failed and trying again.

3D printing is a big part of that process for me. I use it to turn CAD models into physical parts quickly, compare different design iterations and test whether a mechanism actually fits and functions. I enjoy working out tolerances, material choices, wall thicknesses, print orientation and how to make components stronger without adding unnecessary complexity or weight.

Beyond printing, I'm interested in mechanical systems, robotics and the integration of hardware and software. I like working with microcontrollers, wiring, sensors and code, then bringing everything together into a complete prototype. Getting a part to move in CAD is one thing; making it work consistently on a bench is the interesting challenge.

Whether I'm building a robot, repairing hardware or experimenting with a new mechanism, I try to approach the problem with the same mindset: build a version, test it honestly, understand what went wrong and make the next one better.

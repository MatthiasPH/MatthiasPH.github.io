02 / A BIT ABOUT ME

I enjoy making
ideas real.

I like the part of engineering where an idea leaves the screen and you find out if it actually works.

DESIGN→BUILD→IMPROVE

I'm Matthias, a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Auckland. What I enjoy most is the hands-on side of engineering: understanding how something works, developing a design, and turning it into a physical object I can actually test.

3D printing is a big part of that. I use it to move quickly from CAD models to working prototypes, whether I'm checking the fit of an assembly, refining a mechanism, or experimenting with geometry. It's taught me that good design is about much more than how a part looks on screen — clearances, print orientation, material choice and real-world tolerances all matter.

I'm also interested in robotics and the overlap between mechanical design, electronics and software. I enjoy working with microcontrollers, sensors and embedded code, then integrating those components into something functional. My favourite projects involve a mix of CAD, fabrication, troubleshooting and repeated design improvements.

I'm still learning, and that's a big part of why I like engineering. I want to keep building practical skills, working with other people and taking on problems that push me beyond the classroom.

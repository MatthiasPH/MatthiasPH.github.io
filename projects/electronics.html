Electronics / Integration

Modular Electronics Control Board.

A compact, serviceable control board bringing motor-driving and power modules together.

STATUSBuilt
PROJECT SNAPSHOT
TYPEElectronics / Integration
STATUSBuilt

01 / OVERVIEW

The project

I built a control assembly integrating an Arduino Uno, two motor-driver modules, a regulated 5 V buck converter, and removable connectors to replace a larger loose-wiring arrangement.

02 / DESIGN & ENGINEERING

The engineering challenge

Distributing power cleanly, accounting for multiple electrical loads and making the board practical to service and debug.

Approach & contribution

I organised signal routing and power distribution, soldered the connections, and tested the assembly through continuity checks and fault isolation.

03 / OUTCOME

Build & learning

A smaller, more repeatable electronic integration layout with improved service access and easier troubleshooting.

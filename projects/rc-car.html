01 / OVERVIEW

The project

I designed and assembled a custom RC car with a 3D-printed chassis, gearbox and motor mounts, shaft supports and servo steering. It was a practical way to explore gearing, packaging, stiffness and tolerances.

02 / DESIGN & ENGINEERING

The engineering challenge

Packaging the geartrain while maintaining shaft alignment, supporting rotating elements and keeping losses from poor gear mesh under control.

Approach & contribution

I worked through gear ratios and centre distances, designed printed supports and mounts, and revised parts after tests revealed backlash, stiffness and alignment issues.

03 / OUTCOME

Build & learning

A working drivetrain prototype that helped me understand how print orientation and dimensional error translate into real mechanical behaviour.

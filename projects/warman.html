Warman Design Project.

Integrating electronics, control and mechanisms into a constrained university design challenge.

01 / OVERVIEW

The project

Our team developed a machine for the Warman design challenge. My main contributions were the electronics, Arduino-based motor control and rapid prototyping. I also created a detailed CAD robot concept with a mobile chassis, hopper and multi-link gripper; that particular concept was not selected for the final build.

02 / DESIGN & ENGINEERING

The engineering challenge

Coordinating motors and actuators within electrical limits while keeping integration, assembly and troubleshooting manageable.

Approach & contribution

I developed core control functions in C, worked on compact electronic integration, managed concurrent motor operation within current limits, and trialled 3D-printing materials for stronger, lighter parts.

03 / OUTCOME

Build & learning

A physical team prototype and test demonstrations, plus a separate complete CAD concept that taught me about packaging, assembly interfaces and mechanism layout.

04 / MEDIA

The physical team build

Actual photos and a short working demonstration, alongside the separately developed CAD concept.

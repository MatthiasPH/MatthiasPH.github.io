01 / OVERVIEW

The project

I redesigned my first walking robot into a more capable eight-servo platform. Each leg has independent hip and knee movement driven by MG90S servos, with an ESP32-C3 coordinating gait behaviour.

02 / DESIGN & ENGINEERING

The engineering challenge

Getting eight servos to move together without losing usable travel, causing mechanical interference, or overloading the power distribution.

Approach & contribution

I designed and printed the chassis, calibrated servo centres and limits, developed coordinated gait sequences and improved cable routing and sensor placement.

03 / OUTCOME

Build & learning

An assembled second-generation robot with improved foot placement and a more accessible modular platform for testing obstacle detection and future autonomous behaviour.
